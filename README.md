## What the rewards are, and what they aren't

This is where most bonus write-ups get vague, so let's be specific. The Rewards Hub credits most payouts as vouchers and coupons, not as withdrawable USDT. That includes position vouchers usable as futures margin and fee-deduction coupons that offset your trading commissions.

Practically:

- **Position vouchers** give you margin to open futures positions. They show up as trading credit, not as balance you can send to your bank.
- **Fee coupons** knock down what you pay in commissions until they're used up. That's real money saved, but it's saved on trades you were going to make anyway.
- **Campaign tokens** like the GT paid out in some seasonal new-user events are spot tokens credited to your account, which behave like any other holding.

The line to hold onto: a bonus you can't withdraw is only worth something if you were going to trade. If you weren't, you've traded your identity documents and a deposit for coupons you'll never use.

Also check the expiry printed on each voucher. Unclaimed rewards simply vanish at the end of their window, and there's no support ticket that gets them back.

## Common questions

**Can I withdraw the sign-up bonus as cash?**
Mostly no. The rewards are credited as vouchers and coupons — position vouchers for futures margin, fee-deduction coupons for commissions. Some campaign payouts come as spot tokens such as GT, which are ordinary holdings. Read the description on each individual reward before you assume it's cash.

**How long do I have to complete the tasks?**
The Rewards Hub rules state 15 days from registration for the newcomer tasks. Third-party guides often quote 14 days, which looks like an older or region-specific version of the same rule. Treat 15 days as the safe assumption and check the countdown in your own hub.

**Do I have to claim manually?**
The beginner rewards do — click through each completed task. The advanced challenge rewards are distributed automatically after the campaign settles, following a monitoring period.

**Can I register a second account to claim the bonus twice?**
No. Gate treats multiple accounts under the same verified identity as a single participant, and accounts that close and re-register lose new-user eligibility. Attempts to farm rewards across accounts are explicitly listed as a disqualifying offence.

**Is the bonus available in my country?**
The campaign pages state that users in the United States, the United Kingdom and mainland China are excluded for regulatory reasons, and other jurisdictions have partial service restrictions. Institutional, API-only, market maker and affiliate accounts are also excluded.

**Why does the headline figure differ between pages I've seen?**
Because the Rewards Hub is localised. The English and Portuguese versions currently show 118 USDT for the beginner package and 10,000+ USDT for the advanced challenge, the Arabic and Chinese versions show 135 USDT and 15,000 USDT, and other locale versions show up to 165 USDT. Your own hub is the only figure that applies to you.
